Fire Protection: Fire Sprinkler System Installation

Detecting, suppressing and controlling fire hazards in line with Singapore Civil Defence Force regulations.

What is fire protection?

A comprehensive fire protection system safeguards lives, property and operations by detecting, suppressing and controlling fire hazards in compliance with Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) regulations. Our installations deliver reliable fire safety infrastructure for commercial, industrial and residential buildings, with rapid response and peace of mind.

Why choose us

  • CompliantFull adherence to SCDF & SS 575 Code of Practice.
  • SafeDesigned for maximum reliability and occupant safety.
  • InnovativeModern systems using advanced technology and quality materials.

Our fire protection services

  1. Fire Sprinkler System InstallationDesign and install compliant sprinkler systems for all building types.
  2. FM200 Fire Suppression System InstallationClean-agent system for data rooms and sensitive equipment.
  3. Fire Protection Pipework InstallationBlack steel pipework installed to approved standards.
  4. Fire Alarm & Hose Reel SystemsFACP wiring and commissioning, and hose reel cabinets.
  5. Testing and CommissioningPressure testing, flow tests and performance verification.
  6. SCDF Approval and DocumentationSubmission, certification and approval support.
